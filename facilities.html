World-Class Amenities

Engineered for Maximum Study Endurance

Every piece of infrastructure at StudyHub has been thoughtfully chosen to eliminate physical fatigue, maintain mental alertness, and support 8 to 14 hours of continuous preparation.

1. Dedicated Seats

Your assigned workstation is reserved exclusively for your selected time window. No table sharing during your slot, no morning race to grab an open spot, and no table relocations.

2. Comfortable Chairs

High-back ergonomic mesh chairs equipped with adjustable lumbar support, hydraulic height adjustments, and padded armrests to protect your posture during marathon study sessions.

3. Spacious Study Tables

Wide anti-scratch laminate desks with acoustic privacy dividers on three sides, providing plenty of room for your 15-inch laptop, heavy textbooks, and open note registers simultaneously.

4. High-Speed Wi-Fi

Enterprise dual 300 Mbps fiber leased lines with automatic link failover. High-density ceiling access points ensure ultra-low latency for mock test portals and 4K online lectures.

5. Individual Charging Points

Each personal desk includes two universal 3-pin AC power outlets and integrated USB-A / Type-C fast-charge ports with surge protection, keeping your devices charged 100% of the time.

6. CCTV Surveillance

High-definition cameras monitor all corridors, reading bays, locker lockers, and entry points 24/7. Verified biometric access creates a safe and peaceful learning environment.

7. 100% Power Backup

Zero-flicker commercial online UPS combined with an automatic acoustic diesel generator guarantees immediate cut-over during city power cuts. Lights, AC, and Wi-Fi never shut off.

8. Purified Drinking Water

TDS-tested multi-stage RO water purification with touch dispensers for cold, room-temperature, and boiling water. Free herbal tea bags and clean coffee replenishment available.

9. AC & Fresh Air Ventilation

Whisper-quiet inverter air conditioning maintains a crisp 22°C ambient temperature. Dual fresh-air ducting and HEPA air purifiers keep CO2 low, preventing fatigue and drowsiness.

10. Clean & Sanitized Washrooms

Separate male and female washrooms disinfected every 2 hours by dedicated housekeeping staff. Stocked with automated hand dryers, fragrant soaps, and premium paper supplies.

11. 100% Peaceful Environment

Sound-dampened vinyl flooring, anti-reverberation wall fabrics, and a strictly enforced zero-whispering policy create a serene library atmosphere for deep conceptual work.

12. Extended Study Hours

Open 17 hours a day from 06:00 AM to 11:00 PM, 365 days a year including weekends and national public holidays. Study early in the dawn or deep into the night according to your routine.
