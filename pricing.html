Membership & Allocation FAQs

Common queries regarding billing, seat reservations, and shift transitions.

Our system prevents overlapping bookings. If Student 1 books Desk A02 for the Morning Slot (06:00 AM – 12:00 PM), that desk is exclusively theirs for those hours. At 12:00 PM, Desk A02 is sanitized and becomes available for Student 2 who booked the Afternoon Slot (12:00 PM – 06:00 PM). Neither student interferes with the other.
When a student purchases a Full-Day membership, the system locks that specific seat number across ALL time slots (Morning, Afternoon, and Evening). No other student can select or occupy that seat at any point in the day.
